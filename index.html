FAQs

Questions, answered.

Do I have to pay anything to try the service?

No. New clients can be offered one free evaluation lasting 14 calendar days: one Client Folder, up to 3 Data Sources, up to 1,000 Data Rows in each 7-day period and up to 3,000 Data Rows in total. No payment details are needed, and the evaluation ends automatically. It never converts into a paid plan on its own.

To begin, email us and confirm that you accept the Terms & Conditions before uploading any files. The 14 days start once your complete first CSV batch has finished uploading.

What happens when the evaluation ends?

Services stop unless you continue. To keep going, both of us sign a Service Order Form and the initial plan fee is paid. If you need a short bridge while that is completed, we can agree a Temporary Acceptance by email. It is limited in length (2.5 weeks at most) and its scope and any fee are stated in writing.

How do plans and client folders work?

Each plan is bought for one Client Folder, meaning one end client or one separately identified reporting workspace. Need five end clients? Buy five plans. You can mix Light and Standard in any combination, and each folder has its own price, source limit and row limits. Limits can’t be pooled between folders.

Every folder appears in your dashboard under its own Client ID. Buy two Standard plans, for example, and you’ll see two IDs such as C-20214 and C-30213.

Which plan is right for me?

Light ($59) suits a single client’s reporting with up to 3 sources, which is a good fit for white-label work. Standard ($119) gives you up to 8 sources and 3,000 rows a week per folder, and is the one we suggest for an agency-wide overview of ad performance. You can upgrade a folder from Light to Standard at any time.

How is my data stored and protected?

Each client gets a password-protected Proton Drive folder (details sent by email) to store your CSV files. Working copies stay on our own computer. Raw uploads and working copies are deleted at the next Sunday processing cycle, and always within 14 calendar days of upload. Final deliverables and dashboard data are kept for 14 days after delivery, so please download what you want to keep.

Your data is never sold, shared with other clients, used for advertising or uploaded to generative AI tools, and it is never used to train AI models. Longer retention can be agreed in writing and may carry an additional fee.

How does payment work?

Plans are charged in USD, per plan, per monthly billing period, and invoiced in advance. Applicable taxes such as VAT are added where required by law. We don’t charge cards automatically unless you separately agree to recurring payments. RULL.io may be used for payment processing and invoicing only. Your CSV files are never uploaded to it.

Can I cancel?

Yes. You can cancel any one plan, or all of them, at any time by email. We refund 50% of the fee paid for the cancelled plan’s current billing period, and cancelling one plan doesn’t affect the others. Full details are in section 7 of the Terms & Conditions.

Which platforms do you support, and how do I upload the data?

No API access is needed. Download the CSV exports from your platforms, upload them to the secure Proton Drive folder we share with you, and we take it from there. We support Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads, TikTok Ads, Amazon Ads, Microsoft/Bing Ads, Pinterest Ads, X, Shopify and Google Analytics 4, plus other platforms agreed in writing.

What if I go over my plan’s limits?

We’ll talk to you first. Excess files or rows may be deferred to a later period, you may be asked to prioritise material, or we can offer an additional or upgraded plan. Nothing extra is ever charged without your prior written approval.

When will I receive my reports?

Our target is 48 hours after we receive complete, readable files. For the Monday cycle, a complete batch received by 11:59 p.m. on Saturday (in your time zone) is targeted for delivery by 9:00 a.m. on Monday. These are targets rather than guaranteed deadlines, and they pause if we’re waiting on clarification or replacement files.

Do you work with marketing and agency data only?

We specialise in advertising, e-commerce and analytics reporting data. If you have a different reporting dataset, email us and we’ll tell you honestly whether it’s a fit. Please never upload passwords, payment-card data, government IDs or other sensitive personal data.
